# M1: Forms engine (replaces Cognito)

Status as of 29 Oct 2026. Branch `claude/brave-fermat-rb9pan`.

## What M1 delivers

| Area | Where |
|---|---|
| Spec parsing, 24-column layout, steps, expressions, validation, summaries | `lib/spec/*` (pure, unit-tested against all 7 kit specs) |
| Verbatim legal text (T&Cs, waiver) | `lib/spec/content.generated.ts` from `docs/source/tt-forms/content/*.md` (drift-tested) |
| Renderer: every field type, signatures, uploads, checklist, lookup panel | `components/form-engine/*` |
| Workflow engine: `allowedWhen`, status moves, numbering, events, emails, role links | `lib/workflow/*`, `runAction()` |
| Public forms and role links | `/f/<slug>`, `/f/<slug>/thanks`, `/e/<token>` |
| Admin: home, grid, entry page, tasks, export, settings | `/admin`, `/admin/forms/<slug>`, `/admin/forms/<slug>/entries/<n>`, `/admin/tasks`, `/admin/settings` |
| PDFs | `lib/pdf/*`: entry PDF for every form, Delivery Note with T&Cs + waiver + 5 signatures, Deal sheet and collection sheet |
| Encryption | DEAL and Delivery Note `data` encrypted with AES-256-GCM (`ENTRY_ENCRYPTION_KEY`) |
| Schema | `supabase/migrations/0005_forms_engine.sql` |

## Decisions taken in M1 (tell the owner)

1. **RequestReviewChanges keeps Cognito behaviour**: status stays Submitted and the inspector's link opens the entry
   read-only (no action is allowed for the Inspector at Submitted). Confirmed by the owner on 29 Oct 2026.
2. **Public Delivery Note lookup is exact-match and masked** (POPIA). On the public form the rep types the quote no;
   the customer panel shows name, the last 4 characters of the ID number and the city. Staff on `/admin` get the full
   dropdown and the full panel. Cognito showed the full panel publicly.
3. **Notification inboxes are configuration**: the inboxes hardcoded in the specs are seeded into
   `notification_recipients` and editable per form under Form settings. Field tokens such as `[InspectorEmail]` still
   come from the entry.
4. **Email copy kept verbatim**, including "View full entry at CognitoForms.com." in the default bodies (the link now
   points to the platform). Change it when the owner wants.
5. **Deal PDF is provisional**: built from the fields Make fills (BUILD_PLAN §4.1) until the Cognito Word template
   (`content/templates/deal.docx`) is available. Cognito's "edit" document (#2, a fillable PDF) is not reproduced.
6. **Emails are log-only until Resend is configured**: `email_log.status = 'skipped'` with the reason.
7. **Files**: 20 MB per file; allowed types are PDF, JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC and Office documents (checked by
   content, not by name). Photo fields accept images only.

## Kit quirks kept verbatim

Label typos ("Costomer", "COSTOMER DETAILS", "documetns"), Job Card date labelled "Untitled", DEAL `Total` is manual,
Inspection Pass/Fail are independent checkboxes, four Delivery Note signatures are all labelled "Signature",
two Store Room fields are labelled "OTHER", and DEAL keeps Cognito's generic "Action" button (it saves without a status change or email; remove it from the spec if the owner agrees).

## Not in M1 (later phases)

- Turnstile on public forms (M5 hardening). M1 has a honeypot and a per-instance rate limit.
- Cleanup of abandoned uploads under `staging/` (needs the jobs runner): add a daily job that removes staging objects older than 24 h.
- Inviting users and editing roles: one admin now; roles are modelled in `lib/security/can.ts`.
- Cognito history import (M5).
